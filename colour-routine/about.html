Colour
Routine

Same rules. Different outcomes.

A quiet name for a system built around colour, geometry and repetition.

A 5 × 5 geometry. Six colours. One seed.

Each token begins as a seed — a pure number recorded onchain. From that seed the onchain generator applies a fixed set of rules (the square-ring logic, the palette, the opacity variation) and produces the final artwork. Seed, generator, and image all live permanently on the chain. Nothing is left on IPFS. Nothing is deferred.

Like an apple seed planted in the blockchain that grows into an apple tree that bears an apple — still on the blockchain. Everything is linked. Everything is complete.

The local renderer exists only as a parity check: paste the decimal seed and watch the same 5 × 5, six-colour composition appear. The marketplace image should match exactly.

Colour Routine is not a collection of pictures. It is a closed system of colour, geometry and repetition, executed entirely onchain.
