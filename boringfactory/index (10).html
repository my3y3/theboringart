Boring Factory

9×9 pixels on Tezos. Draw, publish, merge into bigger pieces. Fully on-chain SVG — no IPFS.

Create

  • Paint on the 9×9 grid. White = empty background.
  • Toggle Classic (static) or Glitch (sprite bake @ 6fps).
  • Tools: Pen, Erase, Pick color, Fill, Clear. Mirror, opacity, undo/redo.
  • Publish mints an FA2 token; the SVG is stored in the contract.

Merge

  • Pick a layout (1×2, 2×2, …). Fill slots with your 9×9 tokens.
  • Parents lose 1 edition each; a larger on-chain collage is minted.

Cost

  • Platform mint fee: 0. You pay Tezos gas + storage only.
  • Secondary royalty: 8% artist · 2% platform.
